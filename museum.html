Treasures of the Past

Egypt's World-Class Museums

Home to millions of artifacts, Egypt's museums preserve the legacy of one of the world's greatest civilizations.

Egyptian Museum Tahrir

Cairo

The oldest archaeological museum in the Middle East, housing tens of thousands of Pharaonic artifacts including the treasures of Tanis and royal statues.

Grand Egyptian Museum

Giza

The largest museum complex dedicated to a single civilization, showcasing the complete Tutankhamun collection and the colossal Ramesses II statue.

Museum of Islamic Art

Cairo

The world's largest Islamic art museum, featuring rare Quranic manuscripts, Mamluk lamps, and exquisite carpets from across the Islamic world.

Luxor Museum

Luxor

A regional gem with exceptional lighting, displaying statues from the Luxor Temple Cachette and the mummy of Ahmose I.

Nubian Museum

Aswan

A UNESCO-supported museum preserving Nubian heritage, with statues of Kushite kings and immersive dioramas of traditional Nubian life.

Hurghada Museum

Hurghada

The first public-private partnership museum, focusing on "Beauty and Daily Life" through jewelry, cosmetics, and artifacts from various eras.
