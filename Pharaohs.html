The Eternal Legacy

Witness the glory of the pharaohs

Ramesses II (The Great)

Reign: 1279 – 1213 BC

Born: 1303 BC

Died: 1213 BC (age 90)

Dynasty: 19th Dynasty

Achievements: The Battle of Kadesh, Abu Simbel temples, and the Ramesseum.

Tomb: KV7 (Valley of the Kings)

Tutankhamun (The Boy King)

Reign: 1332 – 1323 BC

Born: 1341 BC

Died: 1323 BC (age 19)

Dynasty: 18th Dynasty

Achievements: Restored traditional religion after Akhenaten, famous intact tomb discovery.

Tomb: KV62 (Valley of the Kings)

Hatshepsut (The Female Pharaoh)

Reign: 1479 – 1458 BC

Born: 1507 BC

Died: 1458 BC (age 49)

Dynasty: 18th Dynasty

Achievements: Expedition to Punt, Deir el-Bahri Temple, and prosperous trade.

Tomb: KV20 (Valley of the Kings)

Akhenaten (The Heretic)

Reign: 1353 – 1336 BC

Born: 1380 BC

Died: 1336 BC (age 44)

Dynasty: 18th Dynasty

Achievements: Introduced monotheism (worship of Aten), built the city of Akhetaten.

Tomb: Royal Tomb of Akhetaten (Amarna)

Cleopatra VII (The Last Pharaoh)

Reign: 51 – 30 BC

Born: 69 BC

Died: 30 BC (age 39)

Dynasty: Ptolemaic Dynasty

Achievements: Political alliances with Julius Caesar and Mark Antony, intellectual and cultural revival.

Tomb: Unknown (believed to be near Alexandria)

Khufu (Builder of the Great Pyramid)

Reign: 2589 – 2566 BC

Born: 2620 BC

Died: 2566 BC (age 54)

Dynasty: 4th Dynasty

Achievements: Built the Great Pyramid of Giza, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.

Tomb: Great Pyramid of Giza
